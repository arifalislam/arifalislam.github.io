01Overview

A connected workspace for managing financial operations and control.

JERP Finance & Controlling brings a wide range of finance-related work into one web product. The Figma design maps accounting foundations, bill submission and payment, cost entry, asset management, maintenance, and bank cash accounting. The case study focuses on how those distinct modules can be understood as a connected operational system.

02The Challenge
Many modules, shared financial context

Finance teams work across records, requests, reviews, payments, cost entries, assets, and banking tasks. The design challenge is to make each task discoverable while preserving the relationships between them—for example, connecting a submitted bill to its review and payment, or keeping cost and asset records within the broader finance workspace.

03The Design Direction
Organize around financial work

The board groups related functions into recognizable areas: accounting and currency setup; bill submission, review, and payment; cost management; asset and maintenance operations; and bank cash accounting. This structure gives users a clear way into a large module set without treating every screen as an isolated tool.

04Design Approach

Make complex financial work easier to navigate.

The design work focuses on structuring a broad enterprise scope into clear task areas, then supporting the records and decisions users need within each workflow.

01

Map the modules

Identify the accounting, bills, costing, assets, maintenance, and banking areas represented in the design.

02

Group related tasks

Bring connected steps—such as bill submission, review, and payment—into a legible workflow.

03

Clarify information

Give financial records and operational lists a clear hierarchy for scanning and follow-up.

04

Support decisions

Make review, reconciliation, cost entry, and asset tasks easier to locate and understand.

05

Keep patterns consistent

Use shared interaction and layout principles across the wider JERP enterprise product.

05Solution & Module Scope

A finance system spanning daily operations and control.

01 / Accounting foundation

Set up the financial structure.

Chart of Accounts and Currency Exchange establish key reference areas for finance work.

02 / Accounts bills

Move bills through review and payment.

The design includes Bill Submit, Bill Review, Bill Payment, Conveyance / Other's Bill, and Supplier Advance Request workflows.

03 / Cost management

Record and manage costs.

Manual Cost Input, Batch Cost Management, and RC Cost Entry provide distinct entry points for cost-related tasks.

04 / Assets & maintenance

Track assets and operational upkeep.

Assets Classification and Fixed Assets Management sit alongside Service Center and Vehicle Management.

05 / Bank cash accounting

Support treasury-related work.

Cheque Management, Bank Reconciliation, Bank Facilities, and Loan Management appear in the bank cash accounting area.

06Outcome & Impact

A connected design map for JERP Finance & Controlling.

The supplied Figma file documents a broad FICO module set—from accounting setup and cost entry through bills, fixed assets, maintenance, and banking. The design direction is to make these responsibilities easier to find and follow as part of one enterprise system, establishing measurable operational gains across accounting, bills, and banking workflows.
