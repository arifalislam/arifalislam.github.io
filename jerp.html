01Overview

Designing full Financial & Controlling modules for JERP.

The work paired detailed module design with a scalable design system and reusable component library. The patterns were adopted across the majority of active project areas, helping the product grow with a more consistent foundation.

02The Challenge
Clarity across complex modules

Financial and controlling products bring dense information and connected workflows together. The task was to design the full module set while keeping patterns consistent and reusable across a wider ERP system.

03The Approach
A reusable foundation

I designed complete modules for Financial & Controlling and built a scalable design system with a reusable component library, connecting detailed product work to patterns that could serve other active project areas.

04Design Thinking Process

From user needs to reusable product patterns.

A user-centered sequence keeps complex finance workflows understandable while shaping patterns that can scale across the product.

Complete modules, supported by reusable components.
